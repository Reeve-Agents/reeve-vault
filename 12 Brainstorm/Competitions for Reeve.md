## Recommendation

1. **Build the MVP first.** It unlocks Hackathon ITS (TKT 3–6 prototype required), Imagine Cup, and the 2027 big ones.
2. **Now:** check whether **Hackathon ITS 2026** registration is still open. It is the best national fit, and its Demo Day and business matching in Jan 2027 suits an SME product.
3. **If you are a student:** register interest for **Hult Prize 2027** (business plan, US$1M) and **Imagine Cup 2027** (deadline around 8 Jan 2027).
4. **Calendar for 2027:** DIGDAYA (Feb–Mar), APINDO (by May), Pertamuda (Jul–Sep), Alibaba CoCreate (by Aug). These are the ones with the biggest prizes and the best fit.
5. **Prepare reusable assets:** [[Reeve - Executive Summary]] (business plan base), a 2-minute demo video, a pitch deck, and pilot evidence from 3–5 SMEs.

## Caveats

- Hackathon ITS registration status is unconfirmed (the page renders dynamically).
- Imagine Cup 2027 dates come from a third-party listing, not Microsoft.
- The searches did not surface every local or university competition. Lomba portals (infolomba.id, kabarlomba.com) list many small ones with prizes in the Rp1–30 juta range.
- Student-only competitions (Hult, Imagine Cup, Pertamuda, Gemastik) require enrolled students on the team.
