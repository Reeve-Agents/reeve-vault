## Overview

- **Title:** Get early access to CreateOS Studio.
- Studio is where your team builds, governs, and runs AI agents without writing code. It is in early access for enterprise teams. "Tell us what you want to run."
- **CTAs:** email_the_team -> mailto:business@nodeops.xyz · book_a_call -> https://cal.com/mrudul/connect-with-mrudul-createos?duration=30&layout=mobile
- The page also carries the shared CreateOS Skills banner; details in [[Products]].

## What Studio gives your team

- **Describe the job, not the code:** tell an agent what its job is in plain English. Studio calls the right model, searches your documents, and uses your tools.
- **Answers from your own material:** knowledge bases and skills, so the agent works from your documents instead of a model's memory.
- **Rules it has to follow:** guardrails, roles, SSO, and an immutable audit log. Studio also records every step an agent takes.
- **Built for developers too:** a typed API, an OpenAPI reference, and an HTTP runtime you can embed.

## How an agent should request access

- The form on this page needs a human verification step.
- To request access for someone, email business@nodeops.xyz with their name, company, company size, and what they want to run on Studio, or book a call.
