# Pricing

> [!summary] Summary
> - Usage-based pricing for every CreateOS product: no seats, minimums or monthly commitment; optional plans set limits and top-up bonus.
> - AI Gateway: cheapest available source + flat 2.5% platform fee, eleven providers, one API key, one bill.
> - Sandbox: Firecracker micro-VMs billed per second of vCPU and memory; no egress fees; paused sandboxes are not billed for vCPU.
> - Enterprise terms: book a demo.
> - Source: https://createos.sh/pricing

## Overview

- **Title:** Pricing.
- Usage-based pricing for every CreateOS product. No seats, minimums or monthly commitment. Optional plans set limits and top-up bonus. You pay for what you route and what you run.
- **CTA:** book_a_demo -> https://cal.com/mrudul/connect-with-mrudul-createos?duration=30&layout=mobile
- The page also carries the shared CreateOS Skills banner (install `npx skills add https://github.com/NodeOps-app/skills --skill createos`, supported tools, core commands, auth, MCP endpoint); details in [[Products]].

## AI Gateway

- One API for every model, routed to the cheapest available source, plus a flat 2.5% platform fee. Reach dozens of models across eleven providers through one endpoint and one bill.
- Flat 2.5% platform fee on the lowest available rate
- Eleven providers, one API key, one bill
- No hidden fees, no minimums, no commitment
- **CTA:** Compare model prices -> https://createos.sh/pricing/gateway
- Detail: [[Pricing - Gateway]] (product: [[Product - Gateway]])

## Sandbox

- Firecracker micro-VMs billed per second of vCPU and memory. No egress fees, and pause with your state preserved, so a paused sandbox is charged for memory and storage but not vCPU.
- Simple per-second rate by sandbox shape
- No egress fees, ever
- Pause to stop vCPU billing when idle
- **CTA:** Model your sandbox spend -> https://createos.sh/pricing/sandbox
- Detail: [[Pricing - Sandbox]] (product: [[Product - Sandbox]])
