# Slide 09 - Sustainability

> [!summary] Purpose
> Show measurable environmental impact built on verified data: green SOPs, a Green Auditor, rewards for meeting KPIs, and green-loan readiness, mapped to the SDGs.

## On-slide copy (≤35%)

- Eyebrow: `SUSTAINABLE BY DESIGN`
- Headline: **Greener operations, *proven with real data.***
- Four labels:
  - `Energy & water SOPs`
  - `Green Auditor (CO2e)`
  - `Green Rewards`
  - `Green-loan ready`
- SDG strip (icons only): **7 · 12** (primary), 6 · 8 · 9 · 11 · 13 (secondary)
- Footnote (mono): `Only measured data counts: bills, POS, invoices.`

## Visual (≥65%)

- **Concept:** a cutaway illustration of a **kos building** (rooms as cells):
  - each room shows an electricity and water meter; one room glows with a violet "anomaly" ring ("2× average")
  - a **rooftop solar panel** with a lime production arc
  - a **minimarket shelf** next door with an expiry tag being marked down
- To the right, a **Green Auditor report card**: kWh ↓, m³ ↓, CO2e estimate, `measured` badge, receipt hash, and a lime **"Reward unlocked: −10% next month"** chip. Below it, a small "Lender pack" folder icon.
- Palette: `green`/`mantis` + `signal` on `ink`; SDG icons in official colours, small.

## Layout

- Illustration plus report card ≈ 70%. Headline top-left; SDG strip along the bottom.

## Assets needed

- Kos cutaway illustration, solar panel, shelf/expiry tag
- Report card UI
- Official SDG icons 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13 (from the UN SDG guidelines)

## Speaker notes

"Sustainability is built into the same engine. Energy and water SOPs split bills per room and flag anomalies, like a room using twice the average. Expiry rules mark stock down before it's thrown away. The Green Auditor turns measured bills into CO2e reports with receipts. Businesses that hit their KPIs earn Green Rewards, and the same verified data makes them ready for green loans. We only count measured data from bills, POS and invoices, so it can't simply be typed in. That maps mainly to SDG 7 and 12."
