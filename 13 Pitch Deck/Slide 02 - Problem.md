# Slide 02 - Problem

> [!summary] Purpose
> The audience feels the pain: SMEs run operations by hand and lose money on edge cases, and today's AI agents are too risky to hand that work to.

## On-slide copy (≤35%)

- Eyebrow: `THE PROBLEM`
- Headline: **Small businesses run on chat and spreadsheets.**
- Sub-line: *Automation breaks on edge cases. AI agents can't be trusted with money.*
- Three chips (labels):
  - `Late payments`
  - `Manual mistakes`
  - `Risky AI access`

## Visual (≥65%)

- **Concept:** an "operations chaos" collage around a **kos (boarding house) building** silhouette:
  - WhatsApp chat bubbles ("udah transfer kak?", "bayar minggu depan ya")
  - Excel cells with red highlights
  - sticky notes
  - a double-payment receipt
  - an overdue calendar
- On the right edge, a **faded AI agent glyph holding a key** with a warning outline (`auditor` violet glow turning red-ish): "broad credentials".
- Palette: `ink` background, chaos elements in `white`/grey with a few `signal` red-flag accents. Keep it legible, not noisy.

## Layout

- Collage on the left 8 columns (≈ 65–70%). Headline and chips on the right 4 columns (≈ 30%).

## Assets needed

- Mock WhatsApp bubbles (Indonesian copy), spreadsheet fragment, kos building illustration
- *Optional stat:* `[Number of Indonesian MSMEs — cite BPS/Kemenkop]`. Only show it if sourced.

## Speaker notes

"Take a 30-room boarding house. Rent is billed by WhatsApp, tracked in Excel, chased by hand. Partial payments, double payments, tolerance for long-time tenants: tools like Zapier break on exactly these cases. AI agents could help, but today they act with broad credentials and no proof. On Hacker News this year, builders named broad agent credentials, not sandbox escapes, as the real risk."
