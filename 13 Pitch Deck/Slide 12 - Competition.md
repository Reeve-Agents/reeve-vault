# Slide 12 - Competition

> [!summary] Purpose
> Position Reeve as the only option that both runs operations on its own and proves its work. Show the stack it is built on.

## On-slide copy (≤35%)

- Eyebrow: `WHY REEVE`
- Headline: **Others automate or advise. *Reeve executes and proves.***
- Axis labels:
  - X: `Runs on its own →`
  - Y: `Provable & auditable →`
- Quadrant item labels:
  - `Manual (WA + Excel)`
  - `Zapier / Make / n8n`
  - `Generic AI agents`
  - `Accounting software`
  - **`Reeve`**
- Built-on strip (logos only): CreateOS · Stripe · Xendit/Midtrans · Mera · 0G · Composio

## Visual (≥65%)

- **Concept:** a large **2×2 matrix** on `ink` with hairline axes:
  - Manual: bottom-left
  - Accounting software: bottom-middle (fixed workflows, records but not your SOP)
  - Zapier/Make/n8n: middle-right low (runs, little proof)
  - Generic AI agents: far right, low (runs, no proof)
  - **Reeve: top-right**, glowing `signal` orb with a violet ring
- Bottom: a thin logo strip of the stack.

## Layout

- Matrix ≈ 65%. Headline top-left. Logo strip ≈ 5% at the bottom.

## Assets needed

- Matrix diagram; partner and tool logos (follow brand guidelines)

## Speaker notes

"Manual work is neither automatic nor provable. Workflow tools run steps but don't write tested logic or audit themselves. Generic AI agents act, but with broad access and no proof. Accounting software records, but it doesn't run your SOP. Reeve is the only one that runs on its own and proves every run. It's built on CreateOS sandboxes, Stripe, Indonesian payment rails, Mera passkeys, 0G private inference and Composio connectors."
