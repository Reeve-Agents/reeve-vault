# Slide 10 - Market

> [!summary] Purpose
> Show who Reeve is for first (Indonesian SMEs with recurring, rule-based operations) and how the same engine expands to any business process.

## On-slide copy (≤35%)

- Eyebrow: `WHO IT'S FOR`
- Headline: **Start with Indonesian SMEs. *Expand to any SOP.***
- Ring labels:
  - Core: `Kos · tutoring · clinics · minimarkets`
  - Middle: `Inventory · procurement · HR · support`
  - Outer: `Audit & diligence for companies`
- Placeholder stat (show only when sourced): `[Indonesian MSMEs — cite BPS/Kemenkop]`

## Visual (≥65%)

- **Concept:** **three concentric rings** (glowing outlines on `ink`):
  - **inner ring** in `signal` with small SME icons (boarding house, book, clinic cross, shop)
  - **middle ring** in `mantis` with process icons (box, cart, person, headset)
  - **outer ring** in `auditor` with a building and a magnifier
- A subtle map silhouette of Indonesia behind the inner ring.

## Layout

- Rings ≈ 70% (right/centre). Headline top-left; ring labels attached to the rings.

## Assets needed

- Ring diagram, line icons, Indonesia map silhouette
- Sourced MSME statistic (optional; never invent one)

## Speaker notes

"We start where the pain is sharpest and the data is already digital: Indonesian small businesses with recurring billing, such as boarding houses, tutoring, clinics and minimarkets. The engine isn't finance-specific. Any process with clear triggers, conditions and actions fits, so inventory, procurement, HR and support come next. For larger companies, the Auditor alone becomes an audit and due-diligence product."
