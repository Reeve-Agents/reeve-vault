# Slide 13 - Status and Ask

> [!summary] Purpose
> Be honest about where Reeve is, show the next concrete milestones and how success is measured, introduce the team, and make a clear ask.

## On-slide copy (≤35%)

- Eyebrow: `STATUS & ASK`
- Headline: **Built, piloting next, *measured by real outcomes.***
- Three-step track (labels):
  1. `MVP: billing SOP live` *(update to the true status at pitch time)*
  2. `Pilot: 3–5 Indonesian SMEs`
  3. `Scale: connectors + green mode`
- Metric chips:
  - `Time to first live SOP`
  - `Auditor catch rate`
  - `Collection rate`
- Team: `[Name — role]` × N
- Ask: **`[Funding / pilot partners / credits — fill per event]`**

## Visual (≥65%)

- **Concept:** a **horizontal track** of three milestone nodes (glowing lime for done, outlined for next), with small UI thumbnails (MVP screen, pilot map pins in Indonesia, connector icons).
- Below it, **team avatars** in circular frames (role colour rings).
- Right side: a big **"Ask" card** with a grainy gradient (`volt` → `deep`), holding the ask text.

## Layout

- Track + team ≈ 50%, ask card ≈ 20% (visual block). Text kept under 35% overall.

## Assets needed

- Team photos, MVP screenshot, milestone icons
- The real status and ask, filled in before pitching

## Speaker notes

"[Update before pitching] Today we have [status]. Next we run a pilot with three to five Indonesian SMEs and measure time to the first live SOP, how many planted violations the Auditor catches, and the change in collection rate. Our team: [names and roles]. We're asking for [ask]."
