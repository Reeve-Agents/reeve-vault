# Slide 04 - Solution

> [!summary] Purpose
> Introduce Reeve's three roles clearly: Builder (makes and runs), Auditor (checks and proves), Owner (approves).

## On-slide copy (≤35%)

- Eyebrow: `THE SOLUTION`
- Headline: **Reeve turns your SOP into software *that checks itself.***
- Three role labels (≤ 3 words each, under each figure):
  - `BUILDER`: builds, runs, maintains
  - `AUDITOR`: tests, judges, monitors
  - `OWNER`: approves with passkey

## Visual (≥65%)

- **Concept:** three luminous **glass orbs / tokens** in a triangle, connected by thin lines:
  - **Builder orb** in `signal` lime, with a faint code pattern inside
  - **Auditor orb** in `auditor` violet, with a checkmark/shield pattern
  - **Owner orb** in `white`, with a fingerprint/passkey glyph, at the apex
- Arrows show the loop: Builder → Auditor (submits) → Owner (verdict) → Builder (approval).
- Background `ink`, with a subtle grain gradient behind the triangle.

## Layout

- Triangle centred, ≈ 70% of the area. Headline across the top. Role labels under the orbs.

## Assets needed

- Three 3D/glass orb renders, or flat gradient circles with inner glyphs

## Speaker notes

"Reeve has two agents and one human. The Builder turns your SOP into tested code, runs it and maintains it. The Auditor never writes your rules; it tests them, plants fake violations to prove its checks work, and monitors every run. And you, the owner, approve anything that touches money with your passkey."
