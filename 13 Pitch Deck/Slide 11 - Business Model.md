# Slide 11 - Business Model

> [!summary] Purpose
> Show how Reeve makes money, and that payments work in Indonesia (local rails) as well as internationally (Stripe).

## On-slide copy (≤35%)

- Eyebrow: `BUSINESS MODEL`
- Headline: **Recurring revenue, *paid on local rails.***
- Four stream labels:
  - `Subscription per active SOP`
  - `Usage: compute + AI per run`
  - `Auditor-as-a-service (pay-per-call)`
  - `Partner-funded rewards & lender packs`
- Rail strip (logos only): Stripe · Xendit · Midtrans
- Footnote (mono): `Pricing: hypothesis, validating in pilot.`

## Visual (≥65%)

- **Concept:** a **revenue flow diagram**: four streams (lime, white, violet, green) flow into a central **Reeve core** node. Below it, a **payment rail bar** with the three logos and their roles ("ID: VA · QRIS · e-wallet" under Xendit/Midtrans; "Global + agent payments (MPP)" under Stripe).
- Optional: a small "credits wallet" coin stack showing agents spending from Reeve credits within a mandate.

## Layout

- Flow diagram ≈ 65%. Headline top. Logos small at the bottom.

## Assets needed

- Flow diagram, provider logos (follow each brand's usage rules)

## Speaker notes

"Reeve charges a subscription per active SOP, plus usage for compute and AI on each run, passed through with a margin. The Auditor can also be sold on its own, per call, to other companies and agents through machine payments. Lenders and partners can fund Green Rewards for businesses that hit verified KPIs. In Indonesia we bill and pay through Xendit or Midtrans (virtual accounts, QRIS and e-wallets). Stripe handles international customers and agent payments. Prices are a hypothesis we're validating in the pilot."
