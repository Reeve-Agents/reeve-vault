# Integration - DocuSign

> [!summary] Summary
> - "DocuSign integration for AI agents". Category: Agreements.
> - Agents read executed agreements to extract clauses and obligations, draft documents, and route them for signature, with every send behind approval.
> - Source: https://createos.sh/integrations/docusign

## Overview

- Category: Agreements. Hub listing: "Governed AI across your agreements."
- Calls to action: book a demo, all integrations (https://createos.sh/integrations), platform (https://createos.sh/products).

## The ungoverned risk

- Executed agreements are binding and sensitive.
- An ungoverned agent that can read or send through DocuSign risks leaking contract terms or routing something for signature that should never have gone out.

## What governed agents do

- CreateOS routes DocuSign calls through the governed layer.
- Agents read executed agreements to extract clauses, dates and obligations.
- They draft new documents from approved templates and route them for signature only after a person approves the send.

## What agents do in DocuSign

- Read executed agreements and extract clauses.
- Surface obligations, renewal and expiry dates.
- Draft documents from approved templates.
- Route for signature behind a human approval gate.
- Cite every extracted term to its source page.
- Log every DocuSign call to the audit trail.

## Runs on the unified AI execution layer

- Read access is scoped to the agreements an agent needs.
- Any send routes through human approval.
- Extracted terms are cited to source.
- Every action is logged for legal and compliance review.

## Common questions

- **Can an agent send something for signature on its own?** No. Agents draft and prepare, but any send routes through a human approval gate first, and the action is logged with the agent, the document and the approver.
- **How accurate is clause extraction?** Every extracted clause, date or obligation is cited back to its source page, and outputs run through validation, so a person can verify any term against the original agreement.
- **Is contract data protected?** Access is scoped to the agreements an agent needs, sensitive terms are handled under zero data retention, and every read is logged.
