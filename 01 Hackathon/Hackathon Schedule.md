# Hackathon Schedule

> [!summary] Summary
> - Saturday, October 17, 2026, on Discord. 12:00 to 7:00 PM PT (19:00 to 02:00 UTC).
> - 7 hours: two 15-minute walkthroughs, five and a half hours of building, then an hour of demos and awards.
> - The page shows times in Pacific, with the viewer's local time beside them. Calendar file: https://createos.sh/hackathon/createos-stripe-hackathon.ics
> - Source: https://createos.sh/hackathon#schedule

## How the day runs

| Time (PT) | Time (UTC)¹ | Slot | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12:00 PM | 19:00 | Kickoff | A walkthrough of Stripe Projects from the Stripe team. |
| 12:15 PM | 19:15 | CreateOS Sandbox | What it is and how to use it, with Pratik Balar, Co-Founder and CTO of CreateOS. |
| 12:30 PM | 19:30 | Build (5.5 hrs) | Five and a half hours to take the agent from an empty directory to a demo. |
| 6:00 PM | 01:00 (Oct 18) | Demos and awards | Teams demo what they built; awards go out from the $10,000 prize pool. |
| 7:00 PM | 02:00 (Oct 18) | Wrap | The event ends. |

¹ UTC column derived from the FAQ offset (12:00 to 7:00 PM Pacific = 19:00 to 02:00 UTC).
