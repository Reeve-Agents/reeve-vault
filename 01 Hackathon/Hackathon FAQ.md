# Hackathon FAQ

> [!summary] Summary
> - 8 questions from the "Common Questions" section of the hackathon page.
> - Free to join; needs a free Stripe account. Solo or teams of up to 3. Registration by approval.
> - 12:00 to 7:00 PM Pacific = 19:00 to 02:00 UTC. Submissions go to the Stripe Projects leaderboard.
> - Other questions: hello@createos.sh
> - Source: https://createos.sh/hackathon

## Is it free?
- Yes. The hackathon is free to join.
- You need a free Stripe account; you can create one during setup.

## What do I build?
- An AI agent that writes and runs its own code.
- Set up the stack with Stripe Projects; the agent runs its code inside a CreateOS sandbox.

## Do I have to build one of the ideas on this page?
- No. The ideas are starting points, each built around something CreateOS Sandbox does well.
- Pick one, remix one, or bring your own: any agent that writes code and runs it in a CreateOS sandbox fits the brief.
- Ideas marked Beginner need more than the Free plan's single 1 vCPU sandbox.
- See [[Hackathon Ideas]] and [[Docs - Sandbox Limits]].

## Do I need to know Stripe Projects or CreateOS already?
- No. The first 30 minutes are walkthroughs.
- Stripe Projects from the Stripe team, then CreateOS Sandbox with Pratik Balar, CreateOS's CTO.
- See [[Hackathon Schedule]].

## What time is it where I am?
- Saturday, October 17, 12:00 to 7:00 PM Pacific, which is 19:00 to 02:00 UTC.
- The schedule on the page shows each slot in your own time zone.

## Can I join as a team?
- Yes. Come solo or in a team of up to 3.
- One person registers for the team and adds teammates in the form.

## How does approval work?
- Registration is by approval.
- When you sign up, tell them about yourself and what you'd like to build.
- Once approved, share the Discord link with your teammates.

## Where do I submit?
- Submissions go to the Stripe Projects leaderboard.
- Demos and awards start at 6:00 PM PT on Discord.

## Something else?
- Write to hello@createos.sh.
