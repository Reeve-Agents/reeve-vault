## FAQ
- **Where does our data live?** In your environment. CreateOS runs in your VPC or on-premise, with region-locked compute and zero data retention by default.
- **Is every output defensible?** Yes. Every output cites its source and every action is logged with a full audit trail, so the work holds up under review.
- **Does a lawyer stay in control?** Always. Agents draft, flag, and surface. A lawyer reviews and owns every decision before it is relied on.
- **How fast can we start?** A scoped pilot on one live matter can run in weeks. We forward-deploy engineers to get the first agent into production with you.
- **Can we use CreateOS for arbitration matters?** Yes. We have specific workflows for arbitration record building, evidentiary bundle preparation, and tribunal submission drafting. We support SIAC, ICC, LCIA, DIFC, and ADGM proceedings.
- **Which jurisdictions do you support?** Any jurisdiction where your matter sits. CreateOS processes documents across common law, civil law, and mixed-jurisdiction systems, in English and any language your matter files arrive in. Region-locked deployments are available globally so data stays where it must.
