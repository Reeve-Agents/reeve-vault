# Design Reference Sites

> [!summary] Summary
> - Four sites the user picked as style references. Observed in the browser on 18 Oct 2026 (1440×900 viewport).
> - Shared thread: **dark canvas, one strong hero visual, small precise typography, glow/gradient as light source.** Xerx is the light, editorial counterpoint.

## newform.community

- **What it is:** a creator community (challenges, free mockups, workshops).
- **Observed:** a black canvas; **giant blurred, glowing letterforms** of the "NF" monogram as the hero (violet → lime gradient light); a bold grotesk headline bottom-left ("Be Part Of The NewForm. Learn. Build. Get Discovered."); a short paragraph on the right; a green pill CTA ("Join the community").
- **Take for Reeve:** the cover/hero as a blurred, glowing **"R"** lit by the lime → teal → ink grain gradient; text kept small and bottom-left.

## codapress.co.uk

- **What it is:** a technical publishing house (books such as *Vibe Coding for Beginners*).
- **Observed:** pure black; **monospace / terminal typography** for body copy; minimal nav (BOOKS, INSIGHTS, CONTACT, CLAIM); one hero object (a book cover) centred; lots of restraint.
- **Take for Reeve:** the **glass box**: monospace live logs as a hero visual, one object at a time.

## xerx.io

- **What it is:** a web, software and AI development studio.
- **Observed:** light off-white page; a **large serif headline with a muted italic phrase** ("Websites & software, *built to last.*"); a mono eyebrow ("WEB, SOFTWARE & AI DEVELOPMENT AGENCY"); "Five disciplines, one studio." as **numbered rows (01, 02, 03…) separated by hairlines**, each with a short description and an arrow.
- **Take for Reeve:** headlines with one Instrument Serif italic phrase; the 6-step flow and rule cards as numbered hairline rows.

## agenticwebteam.com

- **What it is:** an AI agent that runs a website ("Your website, run by an AI team that does the work").
- **Observed:** near-black background with **soft green glow blobs**; a pill announcement chip; a big bold headline; mono eyebrows ("ONE AGENT, YOUR JOBS"); **bento cards with giant faded numerals (01, 02)** behind the content; a green primary CTA.
- **Take for Reeve:** the closest positioning cousin ("run by an AI team"); bento cards with faded numerals for the 6 steps; green/lime glow as the "agent at work" light.
