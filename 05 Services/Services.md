## An accountable partner, not a tool vendor

- **The platform under the engagement:** your agents run on CreateOS, the unified AI execution layer built and operated by CreateOS. The engagement ends; the governed layer stays.
- **Embedded, not outsourced:** forward-deployed engineers work inside your team and your environment, not from behind a ticket queue.
- **Governed from day one:** policy enforcement, output validation, and a full audit trail are on from the first call, not bolted on before the audit.
- **Model-agnostic by default:** not locked to any provider. Models are benchmarked against your actual data and swapped without rebuilding your product.
- **Fixed pricing, no surprises:** deliverables, timeline, and cost agreed in writing before the start. No retainer ambiguity, no hourly overruns.
- **You own everything:** all IP, code, and models are yours outright. Everything is documented and your team is trained so you are never dependent on CreateOS.
