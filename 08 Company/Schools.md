## AI education should not stop at awareness

- Every graduating student enters a world where AI is a baseline skill across business, science, commerce, law, design, research, media and entrepreneurship.
- The advantage comes from knowing how to use AI to solve problems, build tools, present ideas and create visible proof of work.
- CreateOS provides a full-year AI Lab in a Box where students move from learning about AI to building with AI.

## The problem schools face

- "AI awareness is everywhere. AI execution is not." Schools should move from AI awareness to AI execution; CreateOS is described as the operating layer that turns a school's AI ambition into student outcomes.
- Most AI programs stop at awareness: theory, awareness sessions and limited demos; students rarely build real applications end to end.
- "Logins everywhere, access nowhere": AI tools spread across platforms, accounts and subscriptions; access breaks when a subscription expires or a platform changes policy.
- Teachers need one guided, unified workspace for the whole building journey (idea to project to deployment) without owning another technical subject.
- Parents and school leaders want projects built, portfolios created, certificates earned and showcases hosted, with recognition from people working in AI.

## What CreateOS delivers

- **Outcomes parents can see:** tangible work every year, and by Grade XII a ready portfolio for college applications, supplementary essays and interviews.
- **Runs alongside CBSE, not against it:** a 2 to 3 hour weekly co-curricular track. CBSE marks come first, always. CreateOS adds, never subtracts. No new subject for teachers to own.
- **Designed the way the brain remembers:** every lesson loops try, reflect, refine, apply. Learn by doing, not by reading.

## What makes CreateOS different

"Not another AI workshop. A full-year building environment."

- **Mentor and Industry Network:** direct access to AI practitioners, product builders, designers and domain experts, with guidance on real-world use cases, project thinking and industry relevance.
- **AI Across Every Stream:** Humanities, Commerce and Science students get equal depth and opportunity; project pathways extend beyond STEM into business, media, law, economics and more.
- **Portfolio and Parent Showcase:** students build real projects they can demonstrate; parent demos and school showcases make outcomes visible.

## What students can build

Six subjects, three project ideas each; the list keeps growing.

| Subject | Project ideas |
|---|---|
| English & Literature | Character chatbots: talk to Hamlet, Elizabeth Bennet, Premchand's heroes. Poetry analyzers and personal reading recommenders. Story generators in Tagore or R.K. Narayan style. |
| History & Civics | What-if historical simulators (what if 1947 didn't happen?). Gamified chapters that make events stick. World history map with lessons embedded. |
| Mathematics | Visual theorem provers and interactive geometry simulators. Personal math tutor that adapts to your weak spots. Statistics dashboard for cricket or IPL scores. |
| Science | Cell division and DNA simulators, chemistry reaction predictors. Plant identifier for biology field trips. Solar system explorer, disease outbreak models for XI to XII. |
| Hindi & Sanskrit | Sanskrit text translators, Bhagavad Gita explainer. Poetry generators in classical meters. Hindi creative writing assistant. |
| Economics & Business | Personal finance tracker, stock market simulator. Local market price predictor for vegetables. Family business automation, demand forecasting for school events. |

- Plus Design, Media, Law and Languages. Students pick the path that maps to what they want to build.
