# Brand Handbook

> [!summary] Summary
> - Logos, colour, type and the rules for using them; "Everything here is cleared for outside use. Use the wording as written."
> - Naming: "CreateOS" (one word, capital C and capital OS); category phrase is "the unified AI execution layer for the enterprise", never "agent operating system".
> - Single accent colour: CreateOS blue #335cff. Fonts: IBM Plex Serif (headlines) and Inter (body).
> - Includes approved boilerplate, voice and tone, vocabulary lists, proof points and claim discipline.
> - Source: https://createos.sh/brand.md (HTML: https://createos.sh/brand)

## Logo

The CreateOS logo is a lockup: the NodeOps symbol followed by the CreateOS wordmark. Use the lockup whole. It is the only approved form of the logo.

| Logo, black | https://createos.sh/createos-logo-black.svg | https://createos.sh/brand/createos-logo-black.png | https://createos.sh/brand/createos-logo-black.pdf |
| Logo, white | https://createos.sh/createos-logo-white.svg | https://createos.sh/brand/createos-logo-white.png | https://createos.sh/brand/createos-logo-white.pdf |

### Do
- Use the full lockup wherever it fits. It is the default.
- Render at 24px tall or larger on screen, 12mm or larger in print.
- Use black on light surfaces and white on dark ones. Nothing else.
- Scale the whole lockup together, at its original proportions.

### Do not
- Do not use the wordmark on its own. It is not a logo without the symbol.
- Do not recolour, add a gradient, outline, shadow or glow.
- Do not stretch, rotate, skew or rebuild the wordmark in another typeface.
- Do not place the logo on a busy image or a colour that leaves it hard to read.
- Do not enclose it in a box or add a tagline underneath.

## Clear space

- Keep clear space on all four sides equal to the height of the symbol.
- Nothing enters that margin: no text, no rule, no edge of a photograph. When in doubt, give it more.

## Naming

- **CreateOS.** One word, capital C and capital OS, never Createos, Create OS or CreateOs. Never all caps except in a logo lockup.
- **The category.** CreateOS is the unified AI execution layer for the enterprise. Use that phrase when the category is named. Do not call it an agent operating system.
- **Product primitives.** CreateOS AI Gateway, CreateOS Sandbox, CreateOS Agents, CreateOS CLI, CreateOS Skills, CreateOS MCP. Each is a governed component of the same execution layer, not a standalone product.
- **NodeOps.** CreateOS is a NodeOps product and shares the NodeOps symbol. Do not present CreateOS as an independent company.

## Boilerplate

### Short
> CreateOS is the unified AI execution layer for the enterprise: routing, governance, output validation, and observability in one governed path from request to response.

### Long
> CreateOS gives enterprises one governed path from request to response. Every call is routed to the right model by task, cost, and latency, checked for prompt injection and policy violations, validated for hallucinations and PII, and logged for audit. Model-agnostic across OpenAI, Claude, Mistral, Gemini, and Meta AI. Sovereign, zero data retention, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified.

## Voice and tone

Direct, technical, commercially grounded. Every systems claim earns its place by being followed within two sentences by a specific number, a named customer, or verifiable proof.

### Use
- Concrete nouns. A 75-day pilot beats a rapid rollout.
- Plain verbs. Verbs do the work, adjectives do not.
- Short claims with the evidence nearby.
- Operational phrasing: build, deploy, ship, route, govern, validate, monitor, audit, scale.
- Honesty about tradeoffs when something has a real cost.

### Avoid
- Hype without proof.
- Generic AI marketing language.
- Overexplaining AI basics to an enterprise reader.
- Heavy metaphors, manufactured urgency, invented quotes.

### Sentence shape
- About 60 percent short sentences of 5 to 12 words, carrying assertions and single facts.
- About 30 percent medium sentences of 13 to 20 words, carrying explanation and evidence.
- About 10 percent longer sentences of 21 words or more, to reset rhythm. Never two in a row.
- Periods over semicolons. Colons are fine for lists. Parentheticals are rare.

## Vocabulary

### Preferred
unified AI execution layer · governed inference · policy enforcement · full lifecycle · route, govern, validate, observe · request to response · model-agnostic · sovereign · zero data retention · region-aware compute · in production · shipped · audit trail

### Do not ship
- AI-powered, AI-driven
- transforms, transformative, revolutionary, game-changing
- next-generation, cutting-edge
- unleash, empower, leverage, synergy, holistic
- best-in-class, world-class, industry-leading
- seamless, streamlined, robust, comprehensive
- end-to-end (write full lifecycle)
- agentic as a buzzword (AI agents as a noun is fine)
- AI deployment used loosely (name the real action instead)
- thrilled to announce, super excited, humbled to share
- in today's fast-paced world, delve into, it's worth noting that
- em dashes (use a period, a comma, or a sentence break)
- exclamation points in product or feature copy

## Claim discipline

Classify a claim before publishing it.

| Kind | Rule |
|---|---|
| Product fact | Must reflect real, shipped product capability. |
| Customer proof | Must come from case-study data, with the customer's agreement to be named. |
| Market statistic | Must carry a source label and a year. |
| Roadmap | Must be phrased as future-facing, for example in progress. |

Never add a customer logo, a revenue, user, or uptime number, or a certification claim without evidence.
