## Featured Founder

- **URL:** https://createos.sh/careers/featured-founder
- **Group / location:** Founders · Remote
- **Summary:** Lead the Build It Now movement: bring an idea to a live session and ship a working app on CreateOS, on camera.
- **Campaign:** "From Idea to Live App in One Session". Build It Now runs live-streamed and recorded sessions where founders and builders turn a rough concept into a working, deployed application in a single session on CreateOS. "We don't do pitch decks; we ship products."
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Pitch and build: bring a business idea or app concept to a recorded or live session.
  - The session: a roughly two-hour build-and-deploy session, online or in person, where the MVP is built and deployed on CreateOS.
  - Champion the tech: explain product requirements and use LLMs to generate PRDs or logic flows during the build.
  - Amplify: share the resulting videos and your live URL with your professional network and social channels.
- **Requirements (who you are):**
  - Builders and prototypers who have built products, apps or MVPs, even only at hackathons.
  - CreateOS user: already has a CreateOS account and has explored the product.
  - AI-fluent: comfortable using LLMs to identify product requirements or generate PRDs.
  - Entrepreneurial: full of ideas, understands product-market fit, wants to drive impact.
  - Social presence: existing social presence and enjoys engaging with the builder community.
  - Community leader (plus): experience leading tech or non-tech communities is a major advantage.
  - Non-technical founders welcome.
- **Commitment and logistics:** a short brief explaining the idea; availability for a roughly two-hour recording session; post-session commitment to share the final content and live app link.
- **Location:** remote. For on-camera shoots the team prioritises candidates in Italy, Singapore, NYC, or India (Delhi and Bengaluru). Online sessions are open globally.
- **Perks:** CreateOS spotlight across NodeOps and CreateOS channels; professional video assets of the build session; CreateOS credits and significant discounts on yearly subscriptions; $100 participation honorarium on completion of the session.
- **Equal opportunity statement:** NodeOps is an equal opportunity partner and encourages applications from underrepresented groups in tech and entrepreneurship.
- **How to apply:** email hiring@nodeops.xyz with subject line "Featured Founder"; attach idea brief, location and availability. Apply link: mailto:hiring@nodeops.xyz?subject=Featured%20Founder

## Forward Deployed Engineer

- **URL:** https://createos.sh/careers/forward-deployed-engineer
- **Group / location:** Engineering · Remote
- **Summary:** Embed with high-value customers to architect and deliver their deployments on CreateOS, and feed product the truth.
- **Warning on page:** not a 9-to-5; no hand-holding, no playbook, no one to escalate to. Wants people who deploy their own agents to get more done.
- **Why now (page theme):** the AI agents era; the gap between idea and production compressing from weeks to seconds; CreateOS "has traction, has revenue" and is building the team to move from a strong product to a category-defining platform.
- **Role:** sits at the intersection of product, engineering and sales; embedded with the highest-value customers and prospects; "not a support role. This is an impact role."
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Embed with enterprise customers and high-growth startups (YC, a16z-backed) to architect and deliver deployments on CreateOS.
  - Build custom integrations, deploy templates and configure workflows that showcase the full capability stack.
  - Be the technical face of CreateOS in pre-sales: live demos, deep infrastructure questions, closing technical blockers.
  - Translate customer pain into documented product gaps; work with core engineering to ship fixes fast.
  - Build reusable deployment templates and playbooks from real engagements.
  - Own technical onboarding for strategic accounts across the full lifecycle.
  - Participate in the #BuildItNow community: workshops, live demos, technical content.
  - Deploy your own agents and AI workflows to multiply output ("We expect it. We reward it.").
- **Requirements, must have:** 3 to 6 years of full-stack or backend engineering (Node.js, Python, Go or equivalent); strong containerization (Docker, Kubernetes) and CI/CD; ability to thrive in ambiguous customer-facing technical environments; cloud infrastructure experience (AWS, GCP or equivalent); can switch between writing code and presenting to a CTO in the same afternoon.
- **Requirements, strong plus:** MCP (Model Context Protocol) or AI-native tooling (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot); GPU compute or AI inference infrastructure; PLG SaaS startup or early dev-tools engineer background; multilingual communication.
- **Compensation and perks:** competitive salary plus meaningful equity; performance-based commission tied to customer deployment success and account expansion; direct line to founders and a seat at the product roadmap table; health benefits, flexible PTO.
- **Location:** Remote.
- **How to apply:** email hiring@nodeops.xyz with subject "FDE - [Your Name]"; include GitHub, a deployment you're proud of, and one paragraph on why infra is the hardest problem in AI.

## Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)

- **URL:** https://createos.sh/careers/site-reliability-engineer
- **Group / location:** Engineering · Remote
- **Summary:** Own the reliability, scale and performance of CreateOS production infrastructure.
- **Warning on page:** not a 9-to-5; no runbook for every incident, no manager between you and production.
- **Why now (page theme):** CreateOS scaling from thousands to millions of deployments; GPU workloads, multi-chain node infrastructure and AI-native applications demanding near-zero downtime; the SRE hired now builds the system and sets the SLO culture.
- **Role:** own reliability, scalability and performance of core deployment infrastructure; own the on-call rotation; direct influence on infrastructure architecture decisions.
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Own reliability, availability and performance of production systems (target: 99.95%+ SLA).
  - Design, implement and maintain the observability stack: metrics, logging, distributed tracing (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry or equivalent).
  - Build and refine the CI/CD pipelines behind the deployment path.
  - Conduct blameless post-mortems and systematically eliminate classes of incidents.
  - Define SLOs/SLIs with product engineering before features ship.
  - Scale Kubernetes clusters supporting GPU compute workloads and AI model inference.
  - Automate toil ("if you do something twice, you write a script; if you do it three times, it is a platform feature").
  - Participate in a 24/7 on-call rotation (fair rotation and an incident bonus structure).
  - Harden security posture: secrets management, network policies, runtime security.
  - Deploy agents for automated runbooks, anomaly detection and incident triage.
- **Requirements, must have:** 3 to 6 years of SRE, DevOps or platform engineering; deep Kubernetes expertise (CKA/CKAD preferred) and container orchestration at scale; proficiency in at least one systems language (Go, Rust or Python); cloud-native infrastructure (AWS, GCP or bare-metal); track record operating production systems at significant scale; incident command comfort.
- **Requirements, strong plus:** GPU cluster management or AI/ML inference infrastructure; node operations (validators, RPC nodes, archive nodes); internal developer platforms or self-service infra tooling; open-source infra contributions.
- **Tech stack:** Kubernetes, Go, Terraform, Prometheus/Grafana, ArgoCD, PostgreSQL, Redis, Cloudflare, AWS/GCP.
- **Compensation and perks:** competitive salary plus meaningful equity; incident bonus structure; direct access to founders and architecture decisions; health benefits, flexible PTO.
- **Location:** Remote.
- **How to apply:** email hiring@nodeops.xyz with subject "SRE - [Your Name]"; include your most memorable incident post-mortem (what broke, what you fixed, what you prevented next time).

## Forward Deployed Strategist

- **URL:** https://createos.sh/careers/forward-deployed-strategist
- **Group / location:** Business · Remote
- **Summary:** Own strategic accounts end to end, from first conversation to close to expansion, as an embedded field operator.
- **Warning on page:** not a 9-to-5; no territory handed to you, no script, no one to ask for permission.
- **Why now (page theme):** enterprise software buying is being disrupted from the bottom up by founders, CTOs and lead engineers at AI-native startups who evaluate tools in hours; the AI agents wave creates a new category of infrastructure buyer.
- **Role:** the most customer-proximate strategic role; "not a sales rep. You are not a consultant"; an embedded operator owning outcomes from first conversation to contract to expansion.
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Own strategic accounts across the full lifecycle: discovery, solution design, close, expand.
  - Identify and pursue partnership opportunities with AI tooling companies (Claude Code, Cursor, emerging LLM players).
  - Build account-specific deployment strategies and present them as business cases.
  - Run velocity demos, a 30-minute founder-led format, to compress deal cycles.
  - Translate field intelligence into GTM playbooks, ICP refinements and competitive positioning.
  - Be the voice of the customer inside CreateOS, feeding market signals to product and BD.
  - Develop and manage a pipeline of 20 to 40 strategic accounts with documented progression in the CRM.
  - Represent CreateOS at key developer conferences (YC Demo Day, AI Engineer Summit, Web Summit).
  - Use AI agents and automation to work the pipeline at scale.
- **Requirements, must have:** 4 to 8 years in BD, strategic sales or consulting at a B2B SaaS or infrastructure company; track record closing $50K to $500K ACV deals; technical fluency (deployment pipelines, APIs, developer workflows); strong async communication; comfort in 0-to-1 environments.
- **Requirements, strong plus:** experience selling into AI-native companies, YC startups or enterprise dev teams; background in developer tools, cloud infrastructure or platform ecosystems; international market experience across the US and Asia-Pacific; PLG to sales-assist motion experience.
- **Metrics owned:** monthly pipeline generated (target: $200K+ MRR pipeline by month 3); demo-to-close rate (benchmark: 25%+); average deal cycle (target: under 21 days for SMB, under 60 days for enterprise); expansion ARR.
- **Compensation and perks:** competitive base salary plus meaningful equity; uncapped commission; performance bonuses tied to pipeline milestones and strategic partnership closures; direct line to founders and product leadership; health benefits, flexible PTO.
- **Location:** Remote.
- **How to apply:** email hiring@nodeops.xyz with subject "FDS - [Your Name]"; include your most complex deal close, your ideal ICP, and one partnership you would pursue for CreateOS on day one.

## Sales Development Representative (SDR)

- **URL:** https://createos.sh/careers/sales-development-representative
- **Group / location:** Business · Remote
- **Summary:** Build the outbound engine from the ground up: prospect, qualify and book demos that convert.
- **Warning on page:** not a 9-to-5; no leads list waiting, no template to copy-paste, no quota hit by doing the minimum.
- **Why now (page theme):** AI developer tools is described as the fastest-growing segment in B2B SaaS; CreateOS has the product, a fast governed deploy path and native MCP integration with Claude Code and Cursor, but no dedicated engine converting awareness into pipeline.
- **Role:** high-velocity outbound; first or second SDR; works with founders and Forward Deployed Strategists; clear path to Account Executive or GTM Lead.
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Own top-of-funnel pipeline generation: prospecting, outreach, qualification, demo booking.
  - Build and execute multi-touch outbound sequences across email, LinkedIn and X (Twitter).
  - Target ICPs: technical founders (YC/a16z-backed), indie hackers, AI startup CTOs, DevOps leads at growth-stage companies.
  - Qualify inbound leads and route them to the right path (self-serve vs. sales-assist vs. enterprise).
  - Manage outreach to 80 to 120 prospects per week with personalised, technically-grounded messaging.
  - A/B test subject lines, messaging angles and CTAs; report weekly.
  - Maintain clean CRM hygiene.
  - Collaborate on outreach assets: battle cards, case studies, objection-handling playbooks.
  - Represent CreateOS at virtual and in-person developer events (hackathons, demo days, community meetups).
  - Use AI agents and automation tools to research prospects, personalise at scale and manage follow-up sequences.
- **Requirements, must have:** 1 to 3 years of outbound sales or BDR/SDR experience, ideally B2B SaaS or dev tools; exceptional written communication; basic technical literacy (deployment, APIs, CI/CD); resilience and velocity; organised, self-driven, async across time zones.
- **Requirements, strong plus:** selling to developer or technical buyers; familiarity with the AI tooling ecosystem (Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Vercel); fluency with global developer communities (multilingual a plus); founder-led sales motion experience.
- **Metrics owned:**

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Outreach volume | 80 to 120 prospects/week |
| Demo booking rate | 8 to 12% of outreach |
| Demo-to-pipeline rate | 60%+ of booked demos |
| Monthly pipeline generated | $50K+ MRR pipeline |

- **Compensation and perks:** competitive base salary plus meaningful equity; uncapped commission on pipeline generated and closed-won; accelerator bonuses for hitting quota early in a month; promotion path to AE within 12 to 18 months of quota attainment; health benefits, flexible PTO.
- **Location:** Remote.
- **How to apply:** email hiring@nodeops.xyz with subject "SDR - [Your Name]"; include one cold email you would send to a YC W26 founder to get them to try CreateOS.

## Developer Relations Engineer (DevRel)

- **URL:** https://createos.sh/careers/developer-relations-engineer
- **Group / location:** Marketing · Remote
- **Summary:** Be the technical face of CreateOS to developers: write code, publish tutorials and build the community.
- **Warning on page:** not a 9-to-5; no pre-written tutorials to re-post, no community to inherit, no marketing team to carry your message; success is measured by deployments rather than impressions.
- **Why now (page theme):** CreateOS entering its growth phase as the MCP ecosystem expands; Claude Code and Cursor users looking for deployment infrastructure that integrates natively; #BuildItNow community has momentum but no dedicated builder.
- **Role:** a technical role with a community multiplier: write code, record demos, publish content, run workshops, and represent CreateOS at YC, a16z events, AI Engineer Summit and developer meetups.
- **Responsibilities:**
  - Technical content and education: build and publish full-lifecycle tutorials, starter kits and deployment guides; create demo apps showcasing MCP integration with Claude Code and Cursor; develop deployment templates highlighting CreateOS primitives; write technical blog posts, comparison guides and benchmark reports (for example "CreateOS vs. Railway: a deployment benchmark"); record video walkthroughs, live coding sessions and YouTube/X content; use AI agents to accelerate content production, translation and distribution.
  - Community and events: own the #BuildItNow community (Discord, X, GitHub Discussions); run monthly virtual hackathons, AMAs and live deploy challenges; represent CreateOS at conferences, YC Demo Days and developer meetups; build and activate a developer ambassador program.
  - Product feedback loop: synthesize community pain points into structured product feedback; triage developer-reported bugs and escalate with reproduction steps; be the voice of the developer in product planning.
- **Requirements, must have:** 2 to 5 years in Developer Relations, technical advocacy or technical content creation; strong software engineering foundation (production-quality code in Node.js, Python or TypeScript); experience deploying and operating web applications (containerization, cloud, CI/CD); exceptional communication; genuine passion for developer communities.
- **Requirements, strong plus:** MCP or LLM-powered developer tooling experience; prior DevRel at a developer-tools, cloud or AI infrastructure company; active presence in developer communities (GitHub stars, technical blog, conference talks, X following); multilingual communication.
- **What success looks like:**

| Goal | Target |
|---|---|
| Published tutorials/guides | 8+ |
| Community members added | 500+ |
| GitHub stars on starter kits | 200+ |
| Demo videos published | 4+ |
| Developer onboarding rate (tutorial to deploy) | 40%+ |

- **Compensation and perks:** competitive salary plus meaningful equity; a platform to build the DevRel presence in AI agent infrastructure; direct line to founders and a seat at the product roadmap table; health benefits, flexible PTO.
- **Location:** Remote.
- **How to apply:** email hiring@nodeops.xyz with subject "DevRel - [Your Name]"; include your best technical tutorial or demo, the community you have built or contributed to, and the first CreateOS tutorial you would write on day one.
