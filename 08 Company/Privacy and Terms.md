## Privacy Notice (https://createos.sh/privacy)

- **Last updated:** February 1, 2026. Issued by "CreateOS by NodeOps" for the website, applications, dashboards, APIs and related services. Forms part of the NodeOps Terms of Service; contact legal@createos.sh.
- **Roles (section 1):** NodeOps may be a controller (analytics, registration, billing, support, fraud prevention, marketing) or a processor for Business Users who control workload data. Third-party providers, networks and wallets may process data as independent controllers.
- **Data collected (section 2):** account and profile; communication and support; billing and transaction (limited payment confirmation and tokens rather than full card numbers); usage, device and log data; security data; wallet and blockchain data; third-party provider interaction; code, configuration and scan-related data; AI and agent feature data (prompts, inputs, outputs; BYOK inputs may also be processed by the third-party model provider); marketing and preferences; information from third parties.
- **Uses (section 3):** provide the services, support and service notices, payments and invoicing, security and fraud prevention, analytics and product improvement (aggregated or de-identified where feasible), opt-in marketing, legal compliance. Lawful bases under GDPR / UK GDPR: contract, legitimate interests, consent, legal obligation, public interest.
- **Sharing (section 4):** vendors and service providers (hosting, analytics, email, support, security, payments); partners or integrations the user chooses; NodeOps entities; law enforcement and regulators where required; corporate transactions. "We do not sell personal data for monetary consideration."
- **Transfers (section 5):** may be transferred internationally; Standard Contractual Clauses or UK Addendum used where GDPR / UK GDPR applies; transfer information via legal@createos.sh.
- **Retention (section 6):** account data kept while the account is active, then deleted or anonymized where feasible; billing records per tax and accounting law; support data as needed; security logs per security needs; marketing suppression records retained to honour opt-outs.
- **Security (section 7):** access controls, logging, encryption in transit and security monitoring; users are responsible for credentials, API keys and wallets.
- **Cookies and analytics (section 8):** essential cookies set automatically; non-essential analytics and marketing cookies need consent where required (EEA/UK); preferences via cookie banner or browser settings.
- **Marketing (section 9):** opt-in newsletters and announcements; unsubscribe via link or legal@createos.sh; suppression lists kept; Business Users must have a lawful basis for employee or end-user data they provide.
- **Rights (section 10):** access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdraw consent, complaint (Office of the Information Commissioner, British Virgin Islands; local EEA/UK authority; additional rights for California and other US states). "We do not sell personal information."
- **Children (section 11):** Services are not intended for anyone under 18; contact legal@createos.sh if minor data was provided.
- **Third-party links and integrations (section 12):** not covered by the notice; wallet addresses and on-chain data are generally public and cannot generally be deleted on-chain.
- **Changes (section 13) and contact (section 14):** may be amended at any time with the "Last Updated" date; questions to legal@createos.sh; identity verification may be requested and requests may be declined where permitted by law.
