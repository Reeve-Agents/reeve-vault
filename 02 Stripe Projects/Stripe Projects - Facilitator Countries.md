# Stripe Projects - Facilitator Countries

> [!summary] Summary
> - Countries where Stripe assumes responsibility for **Indirect Taxes** compliance for Stripe Projects.
> - Stripe or its affiliates act as a marketplace facilitator (as defined under U.S. law), marketplace operator, or electronic interface on your behalf, solely to calculate, collect, and remit indirect taxes.
> - 36 codes in 5 regional groups. Exception: users based in Singapore transacting with providers based in Singapore.
> - Source: https://docs.stripe.com/projects/facilitator-countries
