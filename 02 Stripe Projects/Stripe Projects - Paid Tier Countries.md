# Stripe Projects - Paid Tier Countries

> [!summary] Summary
> - The countries where the **paid tier** of Stripe Projects is supported, listed as ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes by region.
> - 81 codes in 7 regional groups. `US`, `CA`, `GB`, `AU`, `SG` and the listed EU countries are included.
> - Stripe may update the list as needed and advises reviewing the page regularly.
> - Linked from the docs "Upgrade a service tier" section ("See which countries support paid services").
> - Source: https://docs.stripe.com/projects/paid-tier-countries
